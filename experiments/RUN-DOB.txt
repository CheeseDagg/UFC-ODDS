round 8 — the stance sweep, and reach batch 4

WHAT ROUND 7 GOT WRONG, AND WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS.

Round 7 pushed DOB coverage to 97.2% and picked up 1,102 stances along the
way, for free, because ufcstats serves stance on the same detail page as the
date of birth. That looked like a bonus. It was a selection bug wearing a
bonus's clothes.

The only fighters round 7 had a reason to fetch were the ones MISSING a DOB.
After six rounds of backfill that set is not a random sample of the roster —
it is the obscure and the long-retired, the men whose pages nobody had needed
yet. So stance was harvested from precisely the population a southpaw angle
cannot be tested on. Overall stance coverage came out at 12.9%. On the
holdout — bouts from 2023 on, the only bouts that decide anything — it came
out at 1.0%, which is 18 bouts. That is not a thin panel. It is no panel.

This round visits the ~1,280 fighters active since 2015 who have a DOB and no
stance. Those are men round 7 would never have touched, by construction,
because their DOB was already banked. Same request volume as round 7
(~1,300), so it fits inside the existing 38-minute step budget, and
on_partial still banks to disk every 200 details — a wall-clock kill keeps
everything already paid for.

The run reports HOLDOUT stance coverage separately and in full, not tailed.
Overall coverage is not a substitute for it: after round 7 the two numbers
were 12.9% and 1.0%, and the first is the one that would have been reported.

ALSO IN THIS ROUND: reach angles, batch 4.

Five terms, run twice. Raw reach difference; ape index difference; reach as a
prior weighted toward fighters Elo has barely seen; the reach ENVIRONMENT a
man has been fighting into; and reach x age.

The premise is an absorption argument that also explains why nobody should
have expected a reach main effect to show. Elo scores a bout on the rating
difference. If the truth is z = beta * (reach_i - reach_j), then
r_i = beta * reach_i + skill_i reproduces it exactly, and ratings converge
there with no help from anyone. Elo can absorb any per-fighter DIFFERENCE
term, not merely a fixed offset. So a null on raw reach says almost nothing
about whether reach matters in a cage — it says Elo already knows. Only
terms that vary WITHIN a career, or that key on where Elo is not yet
informed, can leave a residual. Raw reach and ape index are in the batch as
CONTROLS: they are the terms that make the other three readable.

The second pass carries raw reach in the baseline. Any interaction built on
reach will happily impersonate the reach main effect if that main effect is
not already spoken for, and an interaction judged without its own main
effect is the main effect in a wig.
