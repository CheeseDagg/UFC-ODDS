round 6 — 2026-07-29

Round 5 named the thing. ufcstats is behind a SHA-256 PROOF-OF-WORK
interstitial: "Checking your browser…", a hand-rolled sha256 in JavaScript,
and a client that has to grind a nonce before it is let through. The cookie
jar came back holding zero cookies, so the cookie is issued only AFTER the
work is done — the browser UA and the jar were necessary but nowhere near
sufficient.

The good news is that this is the most tractable kind of block. It is not a
CAPTCHA and it is not IP reputation; it is arithmetic, and Python has hashlib.
What round 6 needs is the parameter block: the challenge seed, the difficulty,
the cookie name and how the page reloads itself. All of that sits at the END
of the script, and round 5's 120-line log window clipped exactly that off
after spending its budget printing sixty lines of a sha256 implementation we
already have in the standard library.

Round 6 prints the last 2,200 characters instead of the first 6,000, and the
status file keeps 200 lines instead of 120. One more round trip to read the
parameters, then the solver is a dozen lines.
