round 4 — 2026-07-29

Round 3 reported for the first time (that was the point of round 3) and the
report was damning in a useful way: every step said "success", the 26 ufcstats
index pages fetched without a single exception, and the enumeration was ZERO
fighters. Coverage did not move: 4223/8686 before, 4223/8686 after.

A scrape that fetches cleanly and parses to nothing is not a network problem.
LINK_RE required the href to start with `http://`; ufcstats serving `https://`
would produce exactly this signature and no error. Round 4 makes the pattern
scheme-agnostic (https, http, protocol-relative, www or not, any case),
rebuilds the detail URL from the captured fighter ID rather than trusting the
page's spelling of it, and — if a page still parses to zero — logs the body
length, the count of 'fighter-details' mentions and the first 200 characters.

So round 4 either fixes it or says which of the three remaining possibilities
it is: a block page, a moved page, or the regex drifting somewhere else.
