round 5 — 2026-07-29

Round 4's diagnostic did its job and cleared the scraper of the charge. All 26
index letters came back as the SAME 2,994-byte page: `<title>Loading…</title>`,
`robots: noindex`, zero 'fighter-details' anchors. That is a JS interstitial,
not a parse failure and not an outage — ufcstats does not serve the fighter
index to this client at all. The scheme-agnostic regex from round 4 was still
worth having, but it was not the problem.

Two things about the old client made a challenge unpassable by construction:
it announced itself as "CheeseDagg-ufc-odds/1.0", and it discarded cookies
between requests, so a challenge that works by setting one could never have
succeeded. Round 5 sends a browser-shaped header set, keeps ONE cookie jar for
the whole process, and warms it on the events page before touching the index.
Wikidata keeps the honest descriptive UA — WDQS asks for one and throttles
generic browser strings.

And if it is still blocked, round 5 prints the entire ~3 KB body once instead
of the first 200 characters. Whatever the interstitial wants is written in
there. Round 4 spent a whole round trip to read two hundred characters of it.
